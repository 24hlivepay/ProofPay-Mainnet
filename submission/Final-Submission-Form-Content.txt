Project name
ProofPay

One-line description
Multi-asset P2P escrow on Arc Testnet with verifiable delivery and buyer-controlled release.

Project description
ProofPay reduces counterparty risk in peer-to-peer online deals. A buyer creates an escrow, shares a private review link, and locks a selected testnet asset in a smart contract after the seller accepts. The seller confirms delivery on-chain, and the buyer releases payment. ProofPay supports USDC, EURC, and cirBTC escrow contracts on Arc Testnet; Circle email wallets, MetaMask, and Rabby; persistent Neon Postgres order records; buyer and seller dashboards; and Arcscan transaction evidence. The MVP has been exercised through developer-led and ongoing public testing. Automated tests cover the contract happy path, refunds, dispute safeguards, permissions, and invalid inputs. A complete user-facing dispute-resolution workflow is planned for the next version.
