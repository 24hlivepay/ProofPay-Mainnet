PROOFPAY — MID-SUBMISSION CHECKPOINT

Project description
ProofPay is a live P2P USDC escrow MVP on Arc Testnet. It reduces counterparty risk by letting a buyer create a deal, the seller review and accept it with a connected wallet, and the buyer lock test USDC in a smart contract. After the seller confirms delivery, the buyer releases the funds. The public MVP includes MetaMask and Rabby support, persistent order records, pending/active/completed/cancelled states, and Arcscan transaction links. We have completed end-to-end tests across three wallets and are now expanding public community testing. Our next major milestone is Circle User-Controlled Wallets integration to make onboarding more familiar and accessible.
